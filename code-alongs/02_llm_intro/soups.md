**1. Tom Yum Goong (Thai Hot & Sour Shrimp Soup)**  
*A fragrant, spicy, and sour Thai soup brimming with shrimp, mushrooms, and aromatic herbs like lemongrass and kaffir lime leaves, balanced with a hint of heat from chilies.*  
- Ingredients:  
  - 200g fresh shrimp, peeled and deveined  
  - 1 liter water or chicken stock  
  - 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised  
  - 2 kaffir lime leaves  
  - 1–2 Thai bird's eye chilies, sliced  
  - 1 tablespoon lime juice  
  - 1 tablespoon fish sauce  
  - 100g straw or button mushrooms  
  - Fresh cilantro for garnish  
- Steps:  
  1. Bring water to a boil, add lemongrass and kaffir lime leaves, and simmer for 5 minutes.  
  2. Add shrimp and mushrooms; cook until shrimp turns pink and opaque.  
  3. Stir in lime juice and fish sauce, add sliced chilies, and adjust seasoning to taste.  
  4. Garnish with cilantro and serve hot with jasmine rice.  

**2. Miso Soup (Japanese Classic)**  
*A light, umami-rich Japanese staple made with dashi stock and miso paste, often enriched with tofu and wakame seaweed for a comforting, nourishing bowl.*  
- Ingredients:  
  - 4 cups dashi stock (or water with kombu and bonito flakes)  
  - 2 tablespoons white miso paste  
  - ½ block firm tofu, cubed  
  - A small handful dried wakame seaweed, rehydrated  
  - 1 green onion, sliced  
  - Optional: 1 teaspoon soy sauce  
- Steps:  
  1. Gently heat the dashi stock in a pot, avoiding a rolling boil.  
  2. Dissolve miso paste in a ladle of warm stock, then stir gently back into the pot.  
  3. Add tofu and wakame, simmer for 2–3 minutes until heated through.  
  4. Top with sliced green onion and serve immediately, avoiding re-boiling the miso.  

**3. Egg Drop Soup (Chinese Comfort Soup)**  
*A silky, quick Chinese soup where beaten eggs are gently swirled into seasoned broth, creating delicate ribbons of egg, often flavored with ginger and white pepper.*  
- Ingredients:  
  - 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth  
  - 2 large eggs, beaten  
  - 1 tablespoon soy sauce  
  - 1 teaspoon sesame oil  
  - 1-inch piece ginger, minced  
  - ¼ teaspoon white pepper  
  - 1 green onion, sliced  
  - Optional: 2 tablespoons cornstarch slurry for slight thickening  
- Steps:  
  1. Bring broth to a gentle simmer, add ginger and white pepper, and let infuse for 1 minute.  
  2. In a small bowl, whisk the beaten eggs lightly.  
  3. Slowly drizzle the eggs into the simmering broth in a thin, steady stream while gently stirring in a circular motion to form ribbons.  
  4. Drizzle sesame oil and soy sauce, garnish with green onion, and serve hot.
