Hi Bobby! Here's how to get started with trains and fixing them:

- **Learn and train**: Start with the basics of how different trains work, then look for a community college program in railroad or diesel technology. Hands-on apprenticeships with railroads or transit agencies are the best way to learn while earning.
- **Build key skills**: Focus on mechanical repair, electrical systems, hydraulics, welding, and blueprint reading. Safety rules (like FRA regulations) are just as important as the tools.
- **Get hired**: Roles include locomotive mechanic, railcar technician, diesel mechanic, and track worker. Reach out to local rail yards or transit agencies about trainee or helper openings, and consider ASE or FRA certifications as needed.
