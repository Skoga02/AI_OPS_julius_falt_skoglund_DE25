Hey Bobby! To learn about trains and how to fix them, start with trade school or an apprenticeship in diesel technology/rail mechanics. Focus on locomotive or railcar repair, safety (especially around electricity and heavy machinery), and tools like welding and diagnostics. Career paths include Locomotive Technician, Railcar Repairer, or Diesel Mechanic for freight/passenger railroads. Look into union apprenticeships and certifications like CDL or FRA safety. Get hands-on experience, even with small engines, and apply to rail companies or manufacturers.
