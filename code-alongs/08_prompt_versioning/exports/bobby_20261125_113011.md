To learn about trains and how to fix them, start with the basics: study how diesel-electric and electric trains work, learn air brakes, electrical systems, and safety rules (FRA if in the US). Use model trains or a railroad museum for hands-on practice, and consider trade school programs in Diesel Technology or Railroad Technology. For a career, aim for roles like Locomotive Technician, Railcar Repairer, or Diesel Mechanic with rail specialty. Get certifications like ASE diesel, FRA safety, and welding. Look into apprenticeships with Class I railroads (Union Pacific, BNSF) or transit agencies (Amtrak). Your next steps: research local trade schools, tour a rail yard, start studying for ASE diesel tests, and set up job alerts for locomotive technician roles.
